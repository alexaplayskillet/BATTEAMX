HISTORIA DE SULTÁN

Baseball rules control how the game is played, ensuring that it is fair and structured. A regular game lasts nine innings, with each team alternating between attack (batting) and defense (fielding). The batting team aims to score runs by hitting the ball and moving around four bases in order: first, second, third, and home plate.
